How the total is worked out

The ATP ranking counts your best 19 results from the last 52 weeks. Grand Slams and the eight mandatory Masters 1000s (Monte-Carlo is optional) always count. The ATP Finals are added on top of the 19. A result that does not make the cut is shown struck through.

Seeds who get a bye and lose their first match receive the previous round's points (10 at a 96-draw Masters), so pick the round that matches the points you were given.
