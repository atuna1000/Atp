How it works

  • Each Monday is a new ranking week. Events in Playing this week are in progress; their points count from the Monday after the final.
  • Points count for 52 weeks. When an event is played again, last year's result drops off on the same Monday the new one comes in, so defending points matters.
  • The total follows the ATP "best 19" rule: all 4 Grand Slams and the 8 mandatory Masters 1000s always count, plus your best 7 results from ATP 500s, ATP 250s and Monte-Carlo. ATP Finals points go on top. Results outside your best 7 are struck through.
  • Tick Q if you came through qualifying; those points are added to your main-draw points.
  • The calendar repeats each year on the same weeks. Real dates shift a little year to year. Challenger, ITF and United Cup points are not included.
  • Estimated rank uses typical points cut-offs (about 650 points for top 100, 3,500 for top 10). It is a rough guide, not a live ranking.
