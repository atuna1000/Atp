How the total is counted

  • Ranking points follows the ATP "best 19" rule: all 4 Grand Slams and the 8 mandatory Masters 1000s always count (zero if you skipped them), plus your best 7 results from the ATP 500s, ATP 250s and Monte-Carlo. ATP Finals points are added on top as a bonus 20th result.
  • Struck-through points are results that fall outside your best 7 and don't count.
  • Tick Q if you came through qualifying. Your qualifying points are added to whatever you earned in the main draw.
  • If you had a first-round bye and lost your first match, pick the round you lost in. ATP gives that the points of a first-round loss, which the calculator does automatically where it matters.
  • Points tables are the current ATP singles values. Challenger, ITF and United Cup points are not included. Dates are approximate weeks.
